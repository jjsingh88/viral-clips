Privacy Policy — Northstar Viral Clips

Operator: Northstar Crest Holdings LLC ("Northstar," "we," "us")
Effective date: November 15, 2026
Contact: northstarcrestholdings@gmail.com

1. What this policy covers

Northstar Viral Clips is a web tool used only by authorized representatives of Northstar Crest Holdings LLC to manage the company's own TikTok account. This policy explains what information the tool handles and how.

2. Information we receive from TikTok

When a representative signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the tool, TikTok shares:

  • Basic profile information for the authorized account: open ID, username, display name, and avatar image (the user.info.basic scope).
  • An access token that lets the tool upload videos to that account at the representative's direction (the video.upload scope).

The tool does not access any other TikTok user's information, does not read messages, and does not collect follower or following lists.

3. Content uploaded through the tool

Video files a representative chooses in the tool are sent to TikTok through the Content Posting API and published to the company's own TikTok account. We do not use those videos for any other purpose.

4. How we use the information

We use the profile information only to confirm which company account was authorized before allowing uploads. We use the access token only to carry out uploads the representative initiates. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties other than TikTok, as needed to operate the tool.

5. Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored on Northstar's own systems with access limited to authorized representatives. A representative can revoke the tool's access at any time in their TikTok settings, which immediately ends the tool's ability to act on the account. On request by email, we will delete stored account information associated with the tool.

6. Your choices

Authorization is voluntary and can be withdrawn at any time through TikTok settings. Questions or deletion requests: northstarcrestholdings@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted at this URL with its effective date.
